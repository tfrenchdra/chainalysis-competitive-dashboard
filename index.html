AIO Strategic Takeaways (Apr 24, 2026)

🚨 Market Shift: AIO Volatility

TRM Labs lost 78% of AIO traffic in two weeks (8,924 → 1,942/mo), primarily from losing AIO citations on "fiat currency," "fiat money," and related glossary terms. Google's AIO selection is highly volatile — even dominant positions can evaporate quickly between refreshes.

📈 Chainalysis Closing the Gap

Chainalysis AIO traffic grew +22% (1,178 → 1,442/mo) while TRM collapsed. The traffic gap is now 500 monthly visits vs ~7,700 two weeks ago. Momentum is on Chainalysis's side heading into the Q2 content push.

💡 Opportunity: Glossary Still Unbuilt

Chainalysis has no glossary pages in its 118 AIO citations — every hit is a blog post, product page, or homepage. Even with TRM's volatility, their glossary architecture caught 36.8K in AIO-eligible volume. A dedicated Chainalysis glossary targeting "blockchain forensics," "virtual asset service provider," "darknet markets," and "crypto compliance" could produce durable AIO visibility.

✅ Strength: Brand Anchors Holding Firm

Chainalysis's top AIO keywords are branded/product-adjacent and stable: chain analysis (363 traffic), is bitcoin traceable (64), blockchain analysis tools (63), blockchain investigation (62). These won't disappear the way TRM's glossary citations did — they reinforce category authority.

⚠ Elliptic Quietly Gaining

Elliptic AIO traffic grew +36% (819 → 1,115) led by branded queries — "elliptic" (536), "uniccshop.cx" (77), "eliptic" (69) — plus "blockchain analytics" (50). With 56 AIO keywords (vs Chainalysis's 118), Elliptic is over-performing on traffic-per-keyword: 20/kw vs 12/kw for Chainalysis.

🎯 Priority Shared Battlegrounds

22 keywords are shared across 2+ domains in AIOs. The three foundational terms Chainalysis is absent from: blockchain analytics (TRM 169 + Elliptic 50), blockchain forensics (TRM 92 + Elliptic 29), crypto compliance (Elliptic 32). Not being cited on these category-defining queries is the clearest AEO gap to close.

Critical TRM Labs Glossary Strategy Is Capturing High-Volume Keywords

TRM Labs has built a systematic glossary section that now ranks #1 for high-value terms like "fiat currency" (26K vol), "fiat money" (18K vol), "stablecoin" (17K vol), "decentralized finance" (16K vol), and "what is fiat money" (5.6K vol). Their glossary hub (939) plus darknet market (509), blockchain analytics (427), privacy coins (399), and crypto tracing (395) together drive 2,600+ monthly visits. Chainalysis has now launched its own glossary (the hub pulls 564, with pages like crypto mixer at 484 and centralized exchange at 187 starting to rank), though TRM's remains deeper and broader.

5 glossary pages in top 3 ▲ 2,600+ visits from TRM glossary fiat currency: TRM #1, Chain. not ranking

Recommendation: Launch a comprehensive glossary/learn section targeting foundational crypto and blockchain terms. Prioritize "fiat currency," "stablecoin," "decentralized finance," and "blockchain technology" — all 15K+ volume terms where TRM currently owns the SERP.

Warning Chainalysis Traffic Lead Over TRM Narrowed Sharply

Chainalysis's US organic traffic lead over TRM narrowed sharply this month: 19,130 vs TRM's 15,539 (a 3,591-visit lead, down from ~9,600 last month). The homepage anchors the domain at 8,710 US visits, with the glossary hub (564) and a crypto-mixer glossary page (484) now leading the rest. The 14-month trend has flipped: Chainalysis -16% (22,480 → 18,978), TRM +14% (13,422 → 15,320). TRM is closing the gap fast.

US traffic: Chain. 19,130 vs TRM 15,539 ▲ TRM US growth: +14% (14 mo) Chain. US growth: -16% (14 mo)

Recommendation: The viral-spike volatility is itself the lesson — a single piece of timely content moves TRM 50%+ on monthly traffic. Stand up a rapid-response newsjacking pipeline so Chainalysis benefits from the same dynamic. Continue the content-decay audit on declining pages (homepage shed ~2,300 visits MoM earlier, spot-bitcoin-etfs blog softening) to stabilize the long-term trend.

Critical TRM Labs Backlink Acquisition Rate Remains ~3x Faster (by %)

TRM Labs referring domains grew from 5,530 to 11,802 — a 113% increase over 14 months (+6,272 new referring domains). Chainalysis grew from 19,816 to 27,395 (+38%, +7,579 domains). Chainalysis's absolute additions continue to outpace TRM's in raw terms. While Chainalysis still leads on raw count (27,395 vs 11,802), TRM's percentage acquisition rate remains roughly 3x higher. TRM's DR is holding at 76 (+5 over 14 mo), keeping the authority gap at just 7 points.

TRM: +6,272 ref domains (+113%) ▲ Chain: +7,579 ref domains (+38%) DR gap: 7 points (was 11 a year ago)

Recommendation: Invest in digital PR and link-building campaigns to maintain the authority moat. Focus on data-driven research reports and industry studies that naturally attract editorial links. Monitor TRM's backlink sources to identify link opportunities they're capitalizing on.

Warning Chainalysis Top 3 Lead Narrowed This Month

Chainalysis Top 3 keyword count fell to 871 while TRM Labs slipped to 655, so the Top 3 lead narrowed to 216 keywords (from 325 last month). Traffic value of $37.7K still leads TRM's $12.2K (3.1x), but that multiple compressed from 5.4x as TRM's traffic value climbed. Chainalysis still owns the high-commercial-intent queries, but its head-of-page advantage is thinning.

Chain. Top 3 (US): 871 ▼ TRM Top 3 (US): 655 Lead margin: 216 keywords

Recommendation: Reinforce the premium-ranking moat by refreshing the Top 3 keywords with updated 2026 data and stronger internal linking. The traffic-value gap (3.1x lead) is the durable advantage — protect commercial-intent product pages and double down on solution-page SEO.

Warning Elliptic Traffic Keeps Sliding as Viral Hits Wash Out

Elliptic's US organic traffic kept sliding to 2,320 monthly visits (Sep 2026), and its old viral hits have washed out of the top 10 entirely. What's left is evergreen "blockchain basics" content: travel rule (129), what is a DEX (74), and blockchain analytics (68). Elliptic's US traffic is now barely 12% of Chainalysis's. The acquired authority (DR 76, +4,709 ref domains over 14 months) persists, but the traffic keeps falling.

Elliptic Sep 2026 traffic: 2,320 Ref domains: +61% (+4,709) Top page: travel rule (129)

Recommendation: The newsjacking thesis is confirmed multiple times over — Elliptic's deepfake piece, TRM's John Daghita post, and TRM's earlier North Korea analysis all drove five-figure monthly visits from single posts. Develop a rapid-response content engine targeting breaking crypto enforcement news and emerging-tech crime stories. Chainalysis's DR 83 means a comparable post should outrank both competitors if produced within 48 hours of news cycles.

Warning US SOV Lead Compressing Fast as TRM Closes

Chainalysis still leads share of voice, but the gap is closing fast. SOV across the trio: Chainalysis 51.9%, TRM 41.9%, Elliptic 6.2% (Sep 2026). Compared to Aug 2025 baselines (Chainalysis 57.3%, TRM 34.2%, Elliptic 8.6%), Chainalysis is down 5.4 pts, TRM gained 7.7 points, and Elliptic lost 2.4 points. Chainalysis's lead over TRM has compressed from 23 points to 10 in fourteen months. TRM's structural growth has not paused.

Chain. US SOV: 57.3% → 51.9% ▼ TRM US SOV: 34.2% → 41.9% ▲ Elliptic US SOV: 8.6% → 6.2% ▼

Recommendation: Don't let the reclaimed lead create false comfort — the YoY trajectory still favors competitors. Combine three plays: (1) defend traffic-value pages with refresh + schema, (2) launch a glossary section to neutralize TRM's structural advantage, (3) stand up a 48-hour newsjacking workflow on enforcement events. Track monthly SOV and treat any further decline below 40% as a critical incident.

Opportunity Chainalysis Authority Moat Remains Strong — Leverage It

Chainalysis's structural advantages keep widening: DR 83 (vs 76 TRM, 76 Elliptic), 27,395 referring domains (2.3x TRM, 2.2x Elliptic), 19.3M live backlinks (~137x TRM, ~120x Elliptic), and a US traffic value of $37.7K (3.1x TRM, 7.8x Elliptic). The brand query "chainalysis" anchors the homepage at 8,710 US monthly visits. The backlinks profile keeps expanding, with live backlinks up from ~14.5M to 19.3M over the past month, a strong leading indicator of authority even though the traffic-value multiple over TRM has compressed (871 top-3 keywords).

DR advantage: +7 over TRM, +7 over Elliptic Backlink advantage: ~137x over TRM US traffic value: 3.1x TRM Homepage US traffic: 8,710/mo

Recommendation: The authority is there — Chainalysis needs to produce more content to leverage it. A glossary launch, increased blog cadence, and topical hub pages would compound on existing authority. Prioritize content in areas where TRM's glossary is winning with relatively shallow pages that Chainalysis can outperform with deeper, more authoritative content.

Opportunity AEO / AI Citation Positioning

As AI-generated answers (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) increasingly source from authoritative domains, Chainalysis's DR 83 and deep backlink profile position it strongly for AI citation capture. However, this requires content that directly answers common questions in a structured, authoritative format — exactly the type of glossary and FAQ content that TRM Labs is already producing. Without this content layer, Chainalysis risks being overlooked by AI systems in favor of competitors with more comprehensive definitional content.

AI citation potential: High (DR 83) Current AEO-ready content: Low Competitor AEO content: TRM glossary, Elliptic blockchain-basics

Recommendation: Build structured, question-and-answer format content targeting high-volume definitional queries. Add FAQ schema markup to existing blog posts. Create a "blockchain basics" or "crypto compliance guide" section with clear, concise definitions that AI systems can easily extract and cite. Monitor AI citation presence with Ahrefs Brand Radar once configured.
