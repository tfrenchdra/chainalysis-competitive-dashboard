AIO Strategic Takeaways (Apr 24, 2026)

🚨 Market Shift: AIO Volatility

TRM Labs lost 78% of AIO traffic in two weeks (8,924 → 1,942/mo), primarily from losing AIO citations on "fiat currency," "fiat money," and related glossary terms. Google's AIO selection is highly volatile — even dominant positions can evaporate quickly between refreshes.

📈 Chainalysis Closing the Gap

Chainalysis AIO traffic grew +22% (1,178 → 1,442/mo) while TRM collapsed. The traffic gap is now 500 monthly visits vs ~7,700 two weeks ago. Momentum is on Chainalysis's side heading into the Q2 content push.

💡 Opportunity: Glossary Still Unbuilt

Chainalysis has no glossary pages in its 118 AIO citations — every hit is a blog post, product page, or homepage. Even with TRM's volatility, their glossary architecture caught 36.8K in AIO-eligible volume. A dedicated Chainalysis glossary targeting "blockchain forensics," "virtual asset service provider," "darknet markets," and "crypto compliance" could produce durable AIO visibility.

✅ Strength: Brand Anchors Holding Firm

Chainalysis's top AIO keywords are branded/product-adjacent and stable: chain analysis (363 traffic), is bitcoin traceable (64), blockchain analysis tools (63), blockchain investigation (62). These won't disappear the way TRM's glossary citations did — they reinforce category authority.

⚠ Elliptic Quietly Gaining

Elliptic AIO traffic grew +36% (819 → 1,115) led by branded queries — "elliptic" (536), "uniccshop.cx" (77), "eliptic" (69) — plus "blockchain analytics" (50). With 56 AIO keywords (vs Chainalysis's 118), Elliptic is over-performing on traffic-per-keyword: 20/kw vs 12/kw for Chainalysis.

🎯 Priority Shared Battlegrounds

22 keywords are shared across 2+ domains in AIOs. The three foundational terms Chainalysis is absent from: blockchain analytics (TRM 169 + Elliptic 50), blockchain forensics (TRM 92 + Elliptic 29), crypto compliance (Elliptic 32). Not being cited on these category-defining queries is the clearest AEO gap to close.

Critical TRM Labs Glossary Strategy Is Capturing High-Volume Keywords

TRM Labs has built a systematic glossary section that now ranks #1 for high-value terms like "fiat currency" (26K vol), "fiat money" (18K vol), "stablecoin" (17K vol), "decentralized finance" (16K vol), and "what is fiat money" (5.6K vol). Their top 5 glossary pages alone drive 16,100+ monthly visits — nearly 40% of their total organic traffic. Chainalysis has no equivalent glossary or definition content targeting these terms.

5 glossary pages in top 3 ▲ 16,100+ monthly visits from glossary fiat currency: TRM #1, Chain. not ranking

Recommendation: Launch a comprehensive glossary/learn section targeting foundational crypto and blockchain terms. Prioritize "fiat currency," "stablecoin," "decentralized finance," and "blockchain technology" — all 15K+ volume terms where TRM currently owns the SERP.

Critical TRM Labs US Traffic Gap Narrowing — Now Just 9,298 Visits

Chainalysis US organic traffic has declined 29% over 14 months (36,878 → 26,277) while TRM Labs has grown 52% (11,158 → 16,971). The US traffic gap has collapsed from 25,720 visits to just 9,298 — a 64% reduction. At current trajectory rates, TRM Labs continues closing the gap. Chainalysis still leads on Top 3 keywords (584 vs 485) but the absolute lead is shrinking.

US traffic gap: 9,298 ▲ was 25,720 TRM US growth: +52% (14 mo) Chain. US decline: -29% (14 mo)

Recommendation: Audit declining Chainalysis US pages to identify content decay. Refresh high-traffic posts losing rankings and expand content velocity to match TRM's output cadence. Prioritize defending top-traffic pages like the stablecoin and crypto regulation posts.

Critical TRM Labs Backlink Acquisition Rate Is 8x Faster (by %)

TRM Labs referring domains grew from 3,608 to 9,841 — a 173% increase over 14 months (+6,233 new referring domains). Chainalysis grew from 19,540 to 23,830 (+22%, +4,290 domains). While Chainalysis still leads in absolute count (23,830 vs 9,841), TRM's acquisition rate is 1.45x higher in absolute terms and ~8x higher by percentage. Their DR has climbed from 70 to 75 in 14 months, closing the authority gap to just 8 points.

TRM: +6,233 ref domains (+173%) ▲ Chain: +4,290 ref domains (+22%) DR gap: 8 points (was 11)

Recommendation: Invest in digital PR and link-building campaigns to maintain the authority moat. Focus on data-driven research reports and industry studies that naturally attract editorial links. Monitor TRM's backlink sources to identify link opportunities they're capitalizing on.

Warning Chainalysis Top 3 Lead Holding — But Margin Compressing

Chainalysis still leads US Top 3 keyword count at 584 vs TRM's 485 — a 99-keyword lead. While Chainalysis maintains the edge, the multi-quarter trend showed pressure on premium rankings. The current snapshot indicates the position-loss issue stabilized, but vigilance is required: TRM's rapid backlink growth (+173% in 14 months) and content velocity in glossary terms remains a structural threat to top rankings on definitional and educational queries.

Chain. Top 3 (US): 584 TRM Top 3 (US): 485 Lead margin: 99 keywords

Recommendation: Continue position-loss monitoring — identify specific keywords that drop from positions 1-3 to 4-10+. Refresh content with updated data, improved structure, and stronger internal linking. Consider whether competitors are providing more comprehensive or up-to-date answers on these queries.

Warning Elliptic Viral Content Strategy Continues to Drive Spikes

Elliptic's US traffic peaked at 47,515 monthly visits in Jan 2026 — over 10x its Mar 2025 baseline of 4,455 — driven by viral blog content, especially the AI deepfake/undresser article (now 10,508 monthly US visits) and regulatory coverage. While the spike has cooled to 10,909 in Apr 2026, Elliptic's referring domains grew permanently from 7,017 to 11,366 (+62%) over the 14-month window, and US traffic has still grown +145% year-over-year.

Elliptic Jan 2026 peak: 47,515 US visits Ref domains: +62% permanent (+4,349) Top page: 10,508 visits (AI deepfake)

Recommendation: Develop a newsjacking and rapid-response content strategy for trending crypto/regulatory topics. Elliptic's approach of publishing timely, newsworthy analysis on emerging issues generates both traffic spikes and permanent backlink gains. Chainalysis's brand authority positions it well to become the go-to source for breaking crypto compliance analysis.

Warning US SOV Share Declining — Chainalysis Now Under 50%

Chainalysis's share of US organic traffic among these three competitors has declined from 70.3% (Mar 2025) to 48.5% (Apr 2026). TRM Labs grew from 21.3% to 31.3%, and Elliptic surged from 8.5% to 20.1%. While Chainalysis still leads, the trajectory shows a clear convergence pattern. Both competitors are gaining ground simultaneously — at current trajectories the SOV lead will continue eroding through 2026.

Chain. US SOV: 70.3% → 48.5% ▼ TRM US SOV: 21.3% → 31.3% ▲ Elliptic US SOV: 8.5% → 20.1% ▲

Recommendation: This is a compound effect of all the threats above. Addressing the glossary gap, content freshness, and backlink growth rate should stabilize SOV decline. Consider setting a quarterly SOV target and monitoring against it.

Opportunity Chainalysis Authority Moat Remains Strong — Leverage It

Despite the competitive threats, Chainalysis still holds significant structural advantages: DR 83 (vs 75 for both competitors), 24,193 referring domains (2.4x TRM, 2.1x Elliptic), 4.2M live backlinks (~39x TRM, ~35x Elliptic), and a US traffic value of $38.8K (4.7x TRM, 9.3x Elliptic). The brand query "chainalysis" still anchors the homepage at 11,192 US monthly visits. This authority foundation means new content from Chainalysis should rank faster and higher than competitor content — if produced.

DR advantage: +8 over both competitors Backlink advantage: ~39x over TRM US traffic value: 4.7x TRM Homepage US traffic: 11,192/mo

Recommendation: The authority is there — Chainalysis needs to produce more content to leverage it. A glossary launch, increased blog cadence, and topical hub pages would compound on existing authority. Prioritize content in areas where TRM's glossary is winning with relatively shallow pages that Chainalysis can outperform with deeper, more authoritative content.

Opportunity AEO / AI Citation Positioning

As AI-generated answers (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) increasingly source from authoritative domains, Chainalysis's DR 83 and deep backlink profile position it strongly for AI citation capture. However, this requires content that directly answers common questions in a structured, authoritative format — exactly the type of glossary and FAQ content that TRM Labs is already producing. Without this content layer, Chainalysis risks being overlooked by AI systems in favor of competitors with more comprehensive definitional content.

AI citation potential: High (DR 83) Current AEO-ready content: Low Competitor AEO content: TRM glossary, Elliptic blockchain-basics

Recommendation: Build structured, question-and-answer format content targeting high-volume definitional queries. Add FAQ schema markup to existing blog posts. Create a "blockchain basics" or "crypto compliance guide" section with clear, concise definitions that AI systems can easily extract and cite. Monitor AI citation presence with Ahrefs Brand Radar once configured.
